Gramodaya Health Camp: Bogur Village

Following the preliminary village assessment, Gramodaya's first on-ground initiative was the Gramodaya Arogya Shibira (Gramodaya Health Camp) — a free community health check-up held on 26 August 2026, at Bogur Village, Guledakoppa, Dharwad District, organised in collaboration with the Guledakoppa Gram Panchayat and Bogur village administration, under S Cube Foundation's 1000 Lives Health Screening Project.

The camp was graced by chief guest His Holiness Sri Atmarama Mahaswami of Sri Jagadguru Madivaleshwar Kalmath, Garaga. Residents of all ages were offered health check-ups, preventive care, health awareness, community wellness support, and doctor's consultations — with vision testing carried out in partnership with Vasan Eye Care.
