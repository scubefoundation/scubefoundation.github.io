About Us

Building a future where every life has the opportunity to thrive.

S Cube Foundation is a people-first social impact organisation working to create healthier, empowered, and resilient communities.

We believe that compassion should not end with a helping hand. It should create health, opportunity, dignity, and independence.

Why We Exist

Because where you are born should never decide how far you can go.

  • A child deserves the opportunity to learn.
  • A woman deserves the opportunity to become independent.
  • An elderly person deserves to age with dignity.
  • Every individual deserves access to healthcare.

S Cube Foundation exists to turn these beliefs into action.

Our Approach

We don't just help. We build.

We work at the grassroots level to understand real community needs and develop practical, sustainable solutions.

Listen→ Understand→ Act→ Empower→ Sustain

Our goal is not simply to address today's problem, but to help communities become stronger tomorrow.

Founder's Message

A Legacy of Hope

"The true measure of life is not what we achieve for ourselves, but what we leave behind for others."

This belief has lived in my heart since childhood.

Long before S Cube Foundation was established, I carried a dream — not of creating an organization, but of creating hope. I dreamt of building a place where every person, regardless of their circumstances, could find support, dignity, opportunity, and the confidence that they are not alone.

My father dedicated his life to serving society. Although I lost him in a tragic accident, his values never left me. He showed me that the greatest legacy is not wealth or recognition, but a life spent serving others.

If my father gave me the purpose to serve, my mother gave me the strength to pursue it. Through every challenge, she stood beside me with unwavering faith, sacrifice, and resilience. Whatever I become, and whatever S Cube Foundation achieves, will forever reflect the values she nurtured in me.

As I pursued my journey in medicine, I came to understand that healing is about far more than treating illness. Every patient carries a story. Every family carries its own struggles. Medicine taught me to heal where I could, but it also showed me that many challenges begin long before a patient enters a hospital.

Health, education, opportunity, empowerment, compassion, and human dignity are deeply connected. That realization became the foundation of S Cube Foundation.

Our aspiration is not only to respond to today's needs, but to build an institution that continues serving future generations. We envision a future where S Cube Foundation can contribute to hospitals that heal, schools that inspire, centres that empower women, home care that supports children and older adults, and initiatives that strengthen communities and protect our environment.

These are long-term aspirations, and we know that building them will take time, dedication, and the collective efforts of many people.

I do not dream of S Cube Foundation becoming merely a well-known organisation. I dream of it becoming a name people trust.

A name that gives hope.
A name that stands beside people during the most difficult moments.
A name that reminds every person that they are never alone.

My greatest hope is that even 100 years from now, S Cube Foundation will continue to live beyond its founders. I hope that future generations will know it not because of who created it, but because of the compassion it showed, the integrity it upheld, and the countless lives it transformed.

If one day, somewhere, a person in need hears someone say, "Go to S Cube. They will stand with you." Then I will know this dream has fulfilled its purpose.

I invite every individual, volunteer, partner, donor, and well-wisher to become part of this journey. Together, let us build more than an organisation. Let us build a legacy of hope.

With gratitude, humility, and unwavering commitment,
Dr. Sushmita Shinde
Founder, S Cube Foundation
